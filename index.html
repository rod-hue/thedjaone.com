02 / The storyEast Point, GA

A party rocker from the jump.

AOne grew up in a musical family in Atlanta and was on the turntables by twelve. He came out of Tri-Cities High in East Point, the same halls that produced Xscape, OutKast and Jagged Edge, and spent the early 2000s working teen parties and Atlanta clubs.

He interned for Lil Jon, and he was breaking Lil Scrappy and Trillville records in the clubs before either act signed. By seventeen he was on the road with them.

A godbrother booked him for Kandi Burruss's Mother's Day cookout. That gig turned into more than a decade as her DJ and musical director, a co-host seat on Kandi Koated Nights, and the run that put him in front of arenas: The Great Xscape Tour, the Xscape vs. SWV Verzuz, The Queens of R&B Tour and the R&B Music Experience.

"Playing to 20,000 people for the first time surely changed my life."The DJ AOne, to Rolling Out

His heroes are DJ Jelly, Kid Capri and Grandmaster Flash. His job description is simpler. In his words, "I'm a party rocker."

04 / On the roadTours & moments

Arenas, Verzuz, and every city between.

  1. 2026

    Room 112 Tour

    Hosted and DJ'd the 112, Total and Case show at the Tabernacle in Atlanta.

  2. 2024

    The Queens of R&B Tour

    Xscape and SWV co-headline with Mýa, Total and 702. Thirty arena shows, AOne on Xscape's set, and his 40th birthday celebrated at Madison Square Garden.

  3. 2022

    R&B Music Experience

    Billed at FTX Arena in Miami with Keith Sweat, Monica, Tevin Campbell, Tamar Braxton, 112, Silk and Ginuwine, plus the Nashville stop.

  4. 2021

    Xscape vs. SWV Verzuz

    On the decks for the battle, sharing the warm-up with DJ Spinderella.

  5. 2021

    The Millennium Tour

    On the road with Sammie, who opened the 2021 run.

  6. 2019

    The Dungeon Tour

    Kandi's tour with Tamar Braxton, Trina, Plies and Deelishis.

  7. 2018

    Kandi Koated Nights on Bravo

    Co-host of Kandi Burruss's late-night talk show, after years co-hosting the online version. Rolling Out names him to its Master DJ series.

  8. 2017

    The Great Xscape Tour

    Tour DJ with Monica and Tamar Braxton on the bill. Twenty thousand people a night.

  9. 2017

    The Savage Tour

    With Sammie and Tank.

  10. 2010

    Kandi's Back to Me Tour

    The first big tour with Kandi, on the road with Fantasia.

  11. Early 2000s

    Lil Scrappy & Trillville

    Touring professionally at seventeen, after breaking their records in Atlanta clubs.
